# Service Level Agreement

## 1. Availability
The supplier guarantees monthly platform availability of 99.5%, measured
excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

## 2. Response Times
Critical incidents receive a response within one hour. Non-critical incidents
receive a response within one business day.

## 3. Service Credits
Availability below the guaranteed level entitles the customer to a service
credit of 5% of the monthly fee per full percentage point of shortfall.
