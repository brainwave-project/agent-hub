# Employee Handbook — Working Hours

## 1. Standard Hours
The standard working day is eight (8) hours, exclusive of a one-hour unpaid
break. The standard working week is forty hours across five days.

## 2. Overtime
Work performed beyond standard hours is compensated at 1.5 times the base rate
and requires prior written authorisation.

## 3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week subject to manager approval.
