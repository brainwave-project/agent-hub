# Refund Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all purchases made through the online platform.

## 2. Refund Window
A buyer may request a refund of the purchase price within thirty (30) days of
the date of purchase. Requests submitted after this period has elapsed will not
be accepted.

## 3. Submission Procedures
Refund requests are submitted through the designated form and must be
accompanied by the original invoice. Customer service will review the request
within five business days.
