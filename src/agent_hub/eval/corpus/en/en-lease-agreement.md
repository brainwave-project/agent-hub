# Commercial Lease Agreement

## 1. Term
The lease term is one (1) year, commencing on the handover date and renewable
by written agreement of both parties.

## 2. Rent
Rent is payable monthly in advance, no later than the fifth day of each month.

## 3. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement by giving sixty days' written notice.
Early termination by the tenant forfeits the security deposit.
