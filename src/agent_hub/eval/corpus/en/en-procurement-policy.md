# Procurement Policy

## 1. Approval Thresholds
Purchases below 5,000 may be approved by a department head. Purchases at or
above 5,000 require a competitive tender and finance committee approval.

## 2. Vendor Registration
All vendors must be registered in the supplier database and hold a valid
commercial licence before any purchase order is issued.

## 3. Conflict of Interest
Any employee with a personal interest in a bidding vendor must declare it in
writing and recuse themselves from the evaluation.
