# Data Retention Standard

## 1. Retention Period
Personal data collected through service channels is retained for ninety (90)
days from the date of collection, after which it is irreversibly deleted.

## 2. Legal Hold
Records subject to an active legal hold are exempt from scheduled deletion
until the hold is formally lifted.

## 3. Access Logs
System access logs are retained for twelve months and are available to the
security team on request.
