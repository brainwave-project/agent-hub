# Travel Expense Policy

## 1. Daily Allowance
The daily subsistence allowance for domestic travel is 150 per day, covering
meals and incidental expenses.

## 2. Accommodation
Accommodation is reimbursed at actual cost against a receipt, up to the grade
ceiling published annually by finance.

## 3. Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within thirty days of the return date.
Claims submitted late require director approval.
