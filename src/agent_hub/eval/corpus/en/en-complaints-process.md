# Complaints Handling Process

## 1. Submission
Complaints may be submitted through the web form, by email, or in person at
any service centre.

## 2. Acknowledgement and Resolution
Every complaint is acknowledged within two business days and resolved within
fourteen (14) days of receipt.

## 3. Escalation
A complainant dissatisfied with the outcome may escalate to the ombudsman
within thirty days of the final response.
