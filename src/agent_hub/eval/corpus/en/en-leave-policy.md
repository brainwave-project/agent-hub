# Annual Leave Policy

## 1. Entitlement
A full-time employee accrues twenty-one (21) working days of paid annual leave
per calendar year.

## 2. Carry-Over
A maximum of five unused days may be carried into the following year. Days
beyond that limit are forfeited on 31 December.

## 3. Approval
Leave requests must be submitted at least ten working days in advance and
require the direct manager's written approval.
