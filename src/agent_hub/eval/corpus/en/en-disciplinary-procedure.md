# Disciplinary Procedure

## 1. Stages
The procedure comprises three stages: a verbal warning, a written warning, and
termination. Stages are applied in sequence except in cases of gross
misconduct.

## 2. Written Warning
A written warning remains active on the employee's record for twelve months
from the date of issue.

## 3. Right of Appeal
An employee may appeal any disciplinary decision in writing within ten working
days of being notified.
